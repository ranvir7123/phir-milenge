What we built

Phir Milenge

It means “we’ll meet again”, and it’s painted on the backs of Indian trucks.

  • A document editor that runs in any phone’s browser. Nothing to install.
  • Every phone carries the whole doc.
  • When two phones meet, one shows a moving code and the other’s camera catches it. No internet, no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth. A small file works too.
  • They can meet in any order, and everyone ends up with the same doc.

The truck back: a picture and three words made from what the doc says, so anyone can see two phones hold the same doc.
